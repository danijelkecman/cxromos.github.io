# Company Profile Expansion Design

## Goal

Expand CxRomos company-profile copy to represent its work across aerospace,
satellite data, OSINT, risk and finance, markets, banking applications, IoT,
edge devices, and telemetry

## Positioning Structure

Group the capabilities into three connected areas:

- Aerospace intelligence: aerospace operations, satellite data, geospatial systems, routing, and telemetry
- Risk and financial systems: OSINT, market intelligence, risk analysis, and banking and finance applications
- Connected operations: IoT, edge devices, telemetry, real-time platforms, and mobile or offline workflows

This structure avoids turning the company profile into an undifferentiated list
of keywords

## Content Placement

Update:

- Homepage hero, What we build, technical proof, and current focus
- Work page metadata and capability cards
- About page company and founder profile
- Contact invitation
- Footer focus statement
- Default and page-specific SEO descriptions
- README positioning summary

## Terminology

Use `aerospace` instead of `aviation` in broad company-profile positioning

Keep `aviation` where it is historically or technically specific, including
existing insight articles about commercial aviation, cockpit automation, flight
planning, and aviation regulation

Use `risk and finance` or more specific terms instead of the compressed
`risk&finances`

## Style

Preserve the existing concise, systems-oriented voice. Describe capabilities as
operational systems and decision infrastructure rather than generic industry
services

Follow repository slop rules:

- Bullet points do not end with `;` or `.`
- Use `-` instead of an em dash
